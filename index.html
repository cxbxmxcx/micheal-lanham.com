Work with me

Two ways I help teams building with AI agents — one to find out what's wrong, one to make sure it doesn't go wrong again.

Agent architecture reviews

A structured assessment of an agent system you've already built or are about to commit to — where it will hold up, where it will break, and what to do about it.

  • Orchestration and control-flow design
  • Tool and MCP integration patterns
  • Memory, state, and context strategy
  • Evaluation, observability, and failure modes
  • Cost, latency, and model selection

Delivered as a written report with prioritised, actionable findings.

AI agent training workshops

Hands-on team training built from the same material as the books — practical, code-first, and grounded in systems that actually ship.

  • Agent fundamentals and behaviour patterns
  • Multi-agent collaboration and orchestration
  • MCP, tool use, and A2A communication
  • Building evaluation and feedback loops
  • Deployment and production concerns

Remote or on-site. Tailored to your team's stack and experience level. I've previously taught deep learning and reinforcement learning courses through O'Reilly and mentorship programmes.

About

Micheal Lanham is a distinguished software and technology innovator with more than two decades of experience in the industry. He has an extensive background in developing software applications across several domains — gaming, graphics, web development, desktop engineering, AI, GIS, oil and gas geoscience and geomechanics, and machine learning.

At the turn of the millennium he began working with neural networks and evolutionary algorithms in game development — long before either was fashionable. That thread runs through everything since: genetic algorithms and neural architecture search in Evolutionary Deep Learning, generative models in Generating a New Reality, and self-improving, instinctual agent systems in the work currently in development.

His books have been published by Manning, O'Reilly, Apress, Packt and BPB. He lives and works in Calgary, Alberta.
